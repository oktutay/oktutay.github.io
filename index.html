I am a Computer Science student at SoICT, Hanoi University of Science and Technology, a research student at Business AI Lab (BAILab), and a co-founder of Hibiscus Co. My work sits where AI meets security: LLM-based malware family classification, web vulnerability testing, and AIoT security.

I have built no-code/low-code AI workflow platforms, led security-focused product prototypes, and lead AI-in-Security and web vulnerability work at VNPT Cyber Immunity (VCI). I am currently interested in applying machine learning, large language models, and graph-based methods to malware analysis, AIoT security, network security, and AI security.
