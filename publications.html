LLMal: A Robust and Explainable Framework for Malware Family Classification

Dao Anh Tu et al. · fourth author of six

Submitted to the IEEE International Conference on Data Mining (ICDM) 2026, Security Track/Workshop · Under review

LLMal builds a graph representation for each binary and projects it through multiple independent heads, adding a kernel-based decorrelation penalty so predictions draw on many features instead of a small, drift-sensitive subset. Each prediction is traced back to its most influential nodes, and a frozen language model compiles that evidence into a natural-language report. It reaches 97.87% F1 on MalwareBazaar and outperforms the strongest baseline by 40.2% on post-drift MalwareDrift, dropping only 11.62% from its pre-drift score.
